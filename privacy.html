Privacy Policy

01

Information We Collect

When you interact with Vintage Era, you may provide information through contact forms, viewing appointment requests, product inquiries, or restoration service requests.

Depending on how you use the website, this may include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and telephone number.
  • Furniture pieces, products, or collections you inquire about.
  • Preferred viewing appointment dates or times.
  • Details, photographs, or descriptions you voluntarily submit about furniture restoration projects.
  • Other information you choose to include in a message.

Basic technical information may also be processed by the website or its hosting services. The information actually collected depends on the features and services enabled.

02

How We Use Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to product and collection inquiries.
  • Coordinate viewing appointments.
  • Discuss restoration services and project requirements.
  • Communicate with you about a request you have submitted.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • Help protect the website against misuse or security issues.

We should use information only for appropriate purposes connected to your request, website operations, or other purposes explained to you when the information is collected.

03

Information Sharing

Vintage Era does not intend to sell your personal information to third parties.

Information may need to be shared with service providers supporting website hosting, communications, appointment management, or other requested services. It may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, users, or website security.

The actual sharing practices depend on the third-party tools and services used by the live website.

04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

The website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand site usage, or improve the browsing experience.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website features. This policy should be updated to reflect the actual cookies and analytics tools used on the deployed website.

05

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Website security also depends on the hosting environment, integrations, and how submitted information is handled.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage appointments, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Actual retention periods depend on the type of information and the purposes for which it is used.

07

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information held about you. These may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

You can contact us with questions or requests about your information. We may need to verify your request and may be subject to legal exceptions.

08

Third-Party Services

The website may link to external websites or use third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and practices.

Please review the relevant third-party policies when using those services. The privacy practices of external websites are not controlled by this page.

09

Children's Privacy

Vintage Era's website is intended for general audiences and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised as the website, services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page along with a revised last-updated date.

Please revisit this page periodically to review the current policy.

11

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Vintage Era through our contact page.
