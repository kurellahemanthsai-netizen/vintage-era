01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your computer, mobile phone, or other device when you visit it. They help websites remember certain information about your visit and support website functionality.

Some websites also use similar technologies, such as browser storage, tracking pixels, and scripts, to support preferences, analytics, and other functions.

Throughout this policy, the term "cookies" includes cookies and similar technologies unless otherwise specified.

02

How We Use Cookies

Vintage Era may use cookies and similar technologies to support website operation and improve the browsing experience.

Depending on the features and services enabled, they may help us:

  • Keep essential website functions working correctly.
  • Remember selected display or browsing preferences.
  • Understand how visitors interact with website pages.
  • Identify technical issues and improve website performance.
  • Measure the effectiveness of campaigns, where applicable.
  • Support marketing features, where enabled and permitted.

The cookies actually used depend on the functionality, hosting configuration, and third-party services implemented on the live website.

03

Types of Cookies

Cookies can be grouped according to their purpose, source, and duration.

Session Cookies

Session cookies are temporary and generally expire when you close your browser. They can support functionality during a single browsing session.

Persistent Cookies

Persistent cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them. They may help a website remember information between visits.

First-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set by the website you visit and may be used to support its own features.

Third-Party Cookies

Third-party cookies may be set by external services integrated into a website, subject to their configurations and policies.

04

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies and similar technologies may be necessary for core website functions, security, and the delivery of features you request.

Depending on the website configuration, these may support:

  • Basic page navigation and website operation.
  • Security and protection against misuse.
  • Temporary information needed to complete a request.
  • Reliable delivery of requested website features.

Technologies that are strictly necessary may be treated differently from optional cookies under applicable law. We will handle consent requirements according to the rules that apply to our website.

05

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies help websites remember choices you make, allowing certain settings to be retained between visits.

Depending on the features available, these preferences may include:

  • Display preferences, such as light or dark mode.
  • Language or layout preferences, where supported.
  • Other selections intended to make browsing more convenient.

Some Vintage Era settings may be stored using browser storage rather than cookies. Clearing browser storage or changing your browser settings may reset those preferences.

06

Analytics Cookies

If analytics tools are enabled, cookies and similar technologies may help us understand how visitors interact with the Vintage Era website.

Depending on the analytics service used, information may include pages visited, approximate time spent on pages, browser or device information, and general interaction patterns.

These insights may help us improve website navigation, content, usability, and performance.

We do not claim to use a particular analytics provider unless that provider has actually been integrated into the website. Where required by law, consent will be obtained before non-essential analytics technologies are used.

07

Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies and similar technologies may be used if Vintage Era introduces advertising, personalized marketing, or campaign measurement features.

Depending on the services enabled, these technologies may help measure advertising effectiveness or support more relevant promotional content.

Marketing cookies are not necessarily active on our website. If they are introduced, we will provide the disclosures and obtain consent required by applicable law.

08

Third-Party Cookies

Some website features may rely on third-party services. When these services are integrated, their providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Depending on the integrations in use, these services may include:

  • Website analytics and performance tools.
  • Embedded maps or external content.
  • Security, hosting, or technical service providers.
  • Advertising or campaign measurement services, where enabled.

Third-party providers manage their own technologies and practices. We encourage you to review their privacy and cookie policies when using their services.

The specific third parties involved depend on the integrations active on the published website.

09

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, restrict them to selected websites, or remove cookies already stored on your device.

Your available options may include:

  • Blocking all cookies or selected categories.
  • Deleting cookies already stored in your browser.
  • Receiving notifications when websites use cookies.
  • Using available cookie preference controls on a website.

Blocking essential cookies or clearing browser storage may affect certain website features and reset saved preferences.

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies, the relevant consent controls should allow you to make the required choices. This policy page does not itself provide an interactive cookie consent mechanism.

10

Cookie Retention

How long a cookie remains on your device depends on its purpose and configuration. Session cookies generally expire when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain until their expiry date or until you delete them.

Third-party cookie retention periods are determined by the relevant providers. We aim to ensure that technologies used on our website are appropriate for their intended purposes and applicable legal requirements.

Actual cookie names, providers, and expiry periods should be verified against the technologies active on our published website.
